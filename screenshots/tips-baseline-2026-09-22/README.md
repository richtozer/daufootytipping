The screenshots contain personal tipping, points and ranking information.
Observed clipping and sticky-header overlap are existing behaviour to record,
not desired behaviour to reproduce. Standard styling and swipe behaviour should
remain familiar; narrow, wide and large-text layouts need additional validation.
